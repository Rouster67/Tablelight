# Tablelight 1.12.1 — DM screen layout

The DM character screen puts the main controls and character statistics near the top:

- **Start turn**, **Next turn**, **Short rest**, and **Long rest** sit above the portrait and name.
- Character controls sit beside the name, with **Currently displayed** at the upper right.
- **Abilities** replaces **At a glance**, directly above the combat counters.
- Hit points, action, bonus action, reaction, and movement use compact tiles.
- Concentration & conditions sits directly below the counters, followed by ability and feature tabs. Spell slots follow **Currently displayed**
  without a gap, with custom resources underneath them.

Player overlays now show up to fifteen abilities per page. The DM display controls use the same
pages, and existing saved page selections remain navigable. Overlay panels grow to show their content without scrollbars. Conditions show six per page in two rows of three, and custom resources show three per page with Previous/Next controls, including matching DM controls for click-through.

The Custom resources card now has Add, opening a dedicated form. Existing resources remain editable in Edit character. Custom resources display filled/empty charge markers up to a maximum of ten, then current/maximum for larger pools. Conditions, spell slots, and custom resources have clearer borders on the overlay.

The layout adapts to wide and smaller laptop windows while keeping the existing controls.

Use **Setup & help → Updates → Check now**, or download **Tablelight-Setup-1.12.1-x64.exe** from
this release and run it. The installer preserves your saved data and updates the same folder.
The DM title bar and sidebar show **1.12.1** after updating.

This patch keeps save format 10 and remains compatible with 1.12.0 saves and backups. Ability
icons, class themes, player messages, and player HUD controls retain their existing behavior.

The Windows x64 installer remains unsigned. The attached `.exe.blockmap` and `latest.yml` files
support in-app updates; you do not need to open them.
